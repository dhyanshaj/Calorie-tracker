# Nutrition data provenance

`usda-sr28.json` contains unmodified energy (nutrient 208, kcal), protein
(203, g) and total lipid (204, g) per 100 g, extracted from USDA National
Nutrient Database for Standard Reference, Release 28 (2015).

Source archive mirror: https://github.com/alyssaq/usda-sqlite/tree/1e457b599af101ce463a195867574eba16c81acb/data
Original files: FOOD_DES.txt and NUT_DATA.txt. JSON keys are USDA NDB numbers,
not FoodData Central IDs. Official legacy database information:
https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/data-documentation.html

USDA nutrient data is public domain. The mirror is a distribution source,
not an additional nutrition authority. This small bundled dataset is historical,
not a live or comprehensive food database. All 44 records were extracted from
that pinned archive, rather than generated by a language model.

Recipe estimates in `shared/catalog.ts` are calculated from these records:

- Plain roti: 30 g whole-wheat flour + 15 g water per medium cooked roti (45 g).
- Chicken curry: per 100 g edible curry, 60 g cooked skinless chicken thigh,
  25 g tomato, 10 g cooked onion, 5 g olive oil. A bone-in piece is approximated
  as 35 g edible curry/meat with 20 g gravy (55 g total); bones are excluded.
  Real meat yields and oil vary: users should correct this assumption.
- Dal: per 100 g, 90 g cooked lentils, 7 g tomato, 3 g olive oil.

Household portion weights are visible, editable assumptions; they are not claims
that a photograph reveals true weights. Recipe approximations omit spices and
salt (negligible effects on the three tracked nutrients). Added oil/ghee must be
entered separately when beyond the recipe baseline. The optional live USDA
FoodData Central integration returns current database records with their real
FDC ID and source URL; it does not use these NDB IDs as FDC IDs.

## Expanded ingredient and recipe references

The expanded records were extracted from the same pinned archive, not generated
by a model. Downloaded source SHA-256 values:

- FOOD_DES.txt: `bb2047218cdad1d830da69e760386409fde3b7afabceae604c5479f280d4fa2e`
- NUT_DATA.txt: `506c9ee39a276ac8f53565d270597e430944ec3363adb53a91cc6ee8c061fac5`

Added ingredient records include cooked kidney/mung beans, brown rice, millet,
quinoa, besan, semolina, cauliflower, carrots, peas, cucumber, cabbage, orange,
Greek yogurt, peanuts, cashews and dry/raw ingredient records used in recipes.
The catalog contains 22 additional reference recipes; their exact ingredient
weights per 100 g final edible dish are implemented in `addRecipe` calls in
`shared/catalog.ts` and shown in each food's assumption/source. Unallocated
weight is water. Oil-free variants replace the oil with water and retain the
same finished weight; oil explicitly stated by the user is entered separately.

Names such as sambar and bhurji represent simplified reference formulations.
For bhurji the cooked-egg reference is used, so real water loss/frying can differ.
Chickpea dip intentionally omits tahini and is labeled accordingly. Recipes
omit minor spices/salt and are not laboratory analyses or promises of authenticity
for every region. Household serving weights are editable assumptions.

Shopping lists aggregate the reference ingredient weights across planned meals,
including recipe components. Cooked bean/grain weights must not be treated as
dry package weights. No prices are invented; the budget preference filters
specialty ingredients and is not a live grocery-price service.
